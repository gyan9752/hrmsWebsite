How to Compare HR Software for a Growing Indian Business

HRVICO product guide
Updated: October 6, 2026
3 Min Read
At a glance

HRVICO’s published plans list employee management, attendance and leave, employee dashboards, and payroll in selected plans. Before choosing software, compare employee limits and ask vendors to explain payroll coverage, setup terms, privacy safeguards, and data handling.

What HR software can help organize

An HRMS is software for keeping employee information and routine HR workflows together. Depending on the product and plan, this can include employee records, attendance and leave, documents, payroll, reports, and employee dashboards.

HRVICO’s current plan details list employee management, attendance, leave, holiday calendars, reports, and employee dashboards. Payroll Management appears in the Standard and Premium plan details. Review the feature overview and current plan comparison for the published list.

Questions to ask before you choose

  • What’s in each plan? Compare the modules and employee cap with the work your team needs to manage.
  • What does payroll cover? HRVICO lists payroll in Standard and Premium. Its public plan information does not detail statutory calculations, filing outputs, or supported jurisdictions. Ask for the scope you need.
  • How are attendance records handled? HRVICO pages mention location and biometric options. Ask when location is collected, whether biometric images or templates are stored, who can access records, and how long they are retained.
  • What happens during setup? The public materials do not specify setup terms, data migration steps, or response times. Confirm these points directly.
  • What does a listed employee limit mean? Current plans list caps of 5, 25, 50, and 100 employees. The public materials do not explain over-limit behavior, so confirm it before selecting a plan.

Frequently asked questions

Which HRVICO plans list payroll?

Payroll Management is listed in current Standard and Premium plan details. The public plan information does not specify statutory coverage or filing outputs.

What employee limits are published?

The current plan list shows limits of 5, 25, 50, and 100 employees. Check the pricing page for current prices and plan details.

What should I ask about attendance data?

Ask when location is collected, whether biometric images or templates are stored, who can access records, and how long they are retained.

A simple evaluation process

  1. Write down the employee, attendance, leave, payroll, and self-service tasks you want to manage.
  2. Compare those needs against the published plan modules and employee limits.
  3. Ask the provider for written answers about tax treatment, setup, statutory payroll scope, privacy controls, data export, and over-limit behavior.
  4. Use a product demo to review the workflows that matter to your team. A submitted HRVICO demo request is not a confirmed meeting time.

Questions about sensitive attendance data

Public product pages do not explain the collection and retention details for location or biometric attendance data. Health and wellness data handling is not described. Ask HRVICO for its written data-handling details before enabling sensitive features.
