What is HRMS Software?

HRMS (Human Resource Management System) software is a powerful digital solution designed to simplify and automate all core HR operations in a business. In modern businesses, HR departments are expected to handle increasing workloads with accuracy and speed. Traditional manual systems often lead to errors, delays in salary processing, mismanagement of leave records, and poor employee experience. HRMS software solves these problems by bringing automation, accuracy, and transparency into HR operations.

Essential Statutory Compliance Checklist for Indian SMEs (2026)

Non-compliance with Indian labor regulations can lead to severe financial penalties and legal liability. A modern HRMS like HRVico automates the following key compliance mandates:

  • Employees' Provident Fund (EPF): Auto-computes 12% employee and employer share up to ₹15,000 threshold and exports compliant Electronic Challan cum Return (ECR) text files for EPFO upload.
  • Employees' State Insurance (ESIC): Automates deduction of 0.75% (employee) and 3.25% (employer) for staff earning gross monthly salary up to ₹21,000.
  • Professional Tax (PT): Automatically applies state-specific tax deduction slabs across Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh.
  • Income Tax & TDS (Section 192): Supports both Old and New Tax Regimes (Section 115BAC), digital investment proof submissions (80C, 80D, HRA), and auto-generates Form 16 Part A & B.
  • Maternity Benefit & Gratuity: Fully aligned with the Maternity Benefit Amendment Act (26 weeks paid leave) and Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 formula calculations.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is HRMS software and why is it essential for Indian businesses?

An HRMS (Human Resource Management System) is an all-in-one software platform that manages the entire workforce lifecycle—from recruitment and daily biometric attendance to statutory payroll (EPF, ESIC, PT, TDS) and appraisals. For Indian businesses, it eliminates human error and guarantees 100% labor law compliance.

How does HRMS software automate EPF and ESIC calculations?

HRMS software calculates mandatory statutory deductions automatically based on monthly employee attendance and salary structures. It applies 12% EPF contributions up to the statutory ceiling, generates ECR files for EPFO portal submission, and calculates ESIC contributions (0.75% employee, 3.25% employer) for workers within the ₹21,000 wage threshold.

Can HRVico integrate with existing biometric attendance devices?

Yes. HRVico provides open API endpoints and hardware sync utilities compatible with all major biometric devices including eSSL, ZKTeco, and Realtime, enabling real-time punch data synchronization without manual USB exports.

What is the difference between an HRMS and manual Excel spreadsheets?

While spreadsheets require manual data entry, are vulnerable to formula breakage, and lack audit logs, an HRMS automates calculations, secures sensitive financial data with AES-256 encryption, provides self-service employee portals, and reduces payroll processing time from several days to under 10 minutes.

How long does it take to implement HRVico HRMS?

Because HRVico is a cloud-native SaaS platform, implementation typically takes between 24 and 48 hours. Organizations can import existing employee data via standardized Excel templates and go live immediately.

Does HRVico support hybrid and remote employee attendance tracking?

Yes. HRVico includes mobile attendance features with GPS geo-fencing, IP restriction, and optional facial verification, allowing remote and field employees to clock in securely from designated authorized work locations.
